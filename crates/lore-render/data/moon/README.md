# The moon's maps

From NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, "CGI Moon Kit"
(<https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720>), as published there, unchanged:

- `lroc_color_poles_1k.jpg`: the colour of the moon, from the Lunar
  Reconnaissance Orbiter's wide-angle camera (the Hapke-normalised mosaic,
  balanced as an eye sees it), 1024 by 512.
- `ldem_3_8bit.jpg`: its height, from the orbiter's laser altimeter, 1024
  by 512, about 20 km from black to white.

Both are longitude across, with 0 (the middle of the face the Earth sees)
in the middle and east to the right, and latitude down, north at the top.

NASA's content is in the public domain unless noted
(<https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/>). NASA asks for the credit
"NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio" (visualizer Ernie Wright,
scientist Noah Petro).
